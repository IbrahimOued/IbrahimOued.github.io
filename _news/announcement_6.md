## About Vergnet Hydro

Vergnet Hydro is a purpose‑driven tech company on a mission: to make potable water reliable, safe, and accessible for every rural household in West Africa. By combining data‑driven decision‑making, mobile tech, and community‑based maintenance, Vergnet Hydro is turning an age‑old challenge into a modern, scalable solution.

## The Social Impact

* **Thousands of Lives Improved**: With more reliable water systems, communities gain time for education, health, and economic activities.
* **Environmental Sustainability**: Smart monitoring helps conserve water resources and reduces leakages.
* **Economic Growth**: Reliable water infrastructure attracts investment, boosts local businesses, and creates jobs.
* **Scalable Model**: The architecture we build will serve as a template for similar interventions across Africa.
