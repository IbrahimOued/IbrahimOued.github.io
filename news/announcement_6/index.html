Starting a new position as Senior Technical Manager - Digital systems, data & AI at Uduma

Created on August 24, 2025

2025

I am excited to announce that I have joined Uduma as a Senior Technical Manager - Digital systems, data & AI. This marks a big step in my career, and I’d love to explain why this opportunity feels so special – both for me personally and for the people who will benefit from the work we’ll do together. In this role, I will be responsible for designing and implementing robust data architectures and leading integration efforts to ensure seamless data flow across the organization.

About Uduma

Uduma is a purpose‑driven tech company on a mission: to make potable water reliable, safe, and accessible for every rural household in West Africa. By combining data‑driven decision‑making, mobile tech, and community‑based maintenance, Uduma is turning an age‑old challenge into a modern, scalable solution.

My Role at Uduma

My role as a Senior Technical Lead will involve:

Area Key Deliverables Impact
Data Strategy & Architecture • Create a cloud‑native data lake and warehouse architecture.
• Map, model, and secure data from IoT sensors, mobile apps, and legacy systems.
• Draft a 12‑month roadmap for progressive migration.		 A single source of truth that powers all business decisions.
Systems Integration • Build RESTful APIs, sync mobile & web platforms, and orchestrate ETL pipelines with Airflow / dbt.
• Automate data ingestion and quality checks.		 Seamless data flow between on‑field devices and decision‑makers.
AI & Analytics • Deploy predictive models (anomaly detection, flow forecasting) using Python & PyTorch.
• Integrate dashboards (Power BI / Tableau) for real‑time monitoring.		 Anticipate failures, reduce downtime, and optimize water distribution.
Team Enablement • Mentor local tech & data teams across 3 countries.
• Deliver training on data literacy and best practices.		 Empower communities to own and sustain the tech stack.
Governance & Security • Implement data governance, compliance (GDPR‑AF), and role‑based access control.
• Enforce encryption and audit trails.		 Protect sensitive data while maintaining transparency.

For Uduma, this means creating a secure and compliant data environment that fosters trust and accountability.

The Social Impact

  • Thousands of Lives Improved: With more reliable water systems, communities gain time for education, health, and economic activities.
  • Environmental Sustainability: Smart monitoring helps conserve water resources and reduces leakages.
  • Economic Growth: Reliable water infrastructure attracts investment, boosts local businesses, and creates jobs.
  • Scalable Model: The architecture we build will serve as a template for similar interventions across Africa.

Why I Decided to Join

  1. Purpose Over Pay – Working for a company that blends tech with a clear social mission is what fuels my passion.
  2. Hands‑on Innovation – From AI‑driven predictive maintenance to real‑time dashboards, I’ll be at the forefront of cutting‑edge solutions.
  3. Impact‑First Culture – Uduma’s agile, growth‑oriented environment encourages experimentation, learning, and real change.
  4. Regional Reach – I’ll be collaborating with teams in Mali, Burkina Faso, and Côte d’Ivoire, learning from diverse cultures and strengthening my cross‑border leadership skills.
  5. Long‑Term Vision – Beyond the first year, the goal is to institutionalise data‑driven water management so that it scales beyond Uduma’s footprint.
